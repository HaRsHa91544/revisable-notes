# Lecture 5

## Earth Overshoot Day: In a year the day which exceeds the needs of resources than nature can generate. On avg every month of July it occurs.

## Energy Performance Index (EPI): The amount of electricity a building uses by its floor area in a year. Its unit is kWh/m^2/year.

## Life Cycle Assessment (LCA): Evalution of environmetal impacts of product or process in its life cycle rather than looking at final product.

## Urban Mining: Recovering useful materials from discarded products such as electronic waste.
