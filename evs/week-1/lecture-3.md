# Lecture 3

## Types of natural resources
1. Perpetual: Available only for certain period of time. Ex: Solar, Wind, Tide energies.
2. Renewable: Can be reproduced naturally. Ex: Water, soil, plants, animals.
3. Non Renewable: Cannot be reproduced naturally. Ex: Coal, petroleum, minerals.

## Ground water mining: Extracting deep ground water that cannot be renewed within human lifetime.

## Natural Capital = Natural Resources + Natural Services
- Natural Resources: Air, water, land, etc.
- Natural service: Climate Regulation by forest, Air purification by trees.

## Electrical consumption in India:
1. Coal based: 73% of electricity
2. Fossil Fuel based: 75% of electricity
3. Renewable source: 22% of electricity
4. Nuclear power based: 3% of electricity
- Renewables in that breakdown comprised hydropower (11%), wind (5%), solar (5%) and biomass/other renewables (1%).

## Types of cities:
1. Agropolis: A city whose waste is reused by surrounding rural areas.
2. Petropolis: A city which produces more waste than its surrounding rural areas need.
3. Ecopolis: An ecological city whose waste reused within the city.

## Linear Economy: Take -> Make -> Dispose
## Circular Economy: Use -> Recover -> Reuse

## Green Design: Focuses on reducing environmental harm.
## Sustainable Design: Focuses on long term environmental, social and economic impact like Planet, People and Profit.
