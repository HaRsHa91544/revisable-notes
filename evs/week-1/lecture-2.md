## Sustainable Development:
- The definition is taken from the **Brundtland Report, Our Common Future (1987)**.
- The development that meets the needs of present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

## Tragedy of the commons: Individuals overusing a shared resource for their own benefits.

## Population Growth Models:
1. Arithmetic Growth: Population **increases by same number** in every time interval. 
```
Pn = Po + nX

Pn - Population after n intervals
Po - Initial population
X - Fixed increase per interval  
```

2. Geometric Growth: Population **increases by same percentage** in every time interval.
```
Pn = Po(1 + r/100)^n

Pn - Population after n intervals
Po - Initial population
r - Percentage growth rate per interval  
```

3. Exponential Growth: Population **increases by expontential** in every time interval.
```
Pn = Po.e^rn

Pn - Population after n intervals
Po - Initial population
r - Percentage growth rate per interval  
```
