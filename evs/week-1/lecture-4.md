## IPAT Model: It helps us to understand the factors effecting environmental impact.
``
I = P x A x T where
I - Environmental Impact
P - Population
A - Affluence (Consumption per person)
T - Technology impact for consumption
``

## Ecological Footprint: The resources which the humans need and how much waste they generate.

## Biocapicity: Nature's ability to regenerate resources and absorb waste.
- Footprint > Biocapacity: Ecological deficit
- Biocapacity > Footprint: Ecological reserve
