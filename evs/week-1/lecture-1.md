# Lecture 1

## Domains of Atmosphere:
1. Atmosphere - Air
2. Hydrosphere - Water
3. Lithosphere - Land or Soil
4. Biosphere - Living organisms

## Environmental concerns:
- Air quality - Industrial or vehicle emissions, stubble burning
- Water quantity & quality - Drinking water shortage, wastewater discharge
- Soil Quality - Erosion, Nutrient depletion, contamination by chemicals
- Biodiversity - Species extinction, decline ecosystem stability

## Urban heat island effect: Urban area can be hotter than the surrounded rural areas.

## Bio accumulation: The process of building up of chemical substance inside an organism over the period of time which effects the other living organisms through ecological pyramid.

## Pollutant: A chemical or substance that causes harmful effects on humans or other organisms.

## Contaminant: A substance present where it should not be or at a higher concentration above its normal level.
