# Lecture 21

## Renewable Energy: The energy which can be replenished or refilled by natural process at a rate equal to or greater than their use.

## Three goals of energy system:
1. Security
2. Affordability
3. Sustainability

## Types of renewable energy
1. Solar energy
2. Wind energy
3. Geothermal energy
4. Hydropower energy
5. Bioenergy

## Renewable energy potential
1. Total potential - Amount of energy present in nature
2. Technical potential - Feasiblity to obtain energy using technology
3. Economic potential - Spending money to implement technology
4. Market potential - Generates revenue or profit in market

## Energy conversion and efficiency
### Energy passes through a chain: Primary energy -> Energy carrier -> Energy service
#### Ex: Sunlight -> Electricity -> Lighting
### People need energy service not primary energy.

## Intermittency: Solar and wind are intermittent means their output varies with sunlight and wind availability.

## Energy Storage: Storing the intermittent energies helps to use them on demand. 
### Storage Technologies:
1. Pumped hydro
2. Compressed air storage
3. Flywheel
4. Batteries
5. Supercapacitors
6. Superconducting magnetic storage
7. Thermal energy storage
