# Lecture 14

## Transboundary Air Pollution: When air pollutants from one country travelling another country. Ex: Transboundary smoke haze, acid deposition.

## Transboundary smoke haze: Smoke and fine particles from burning forest travel across countries.

## ASEAN Agreement on Transboundary smoke haze:
- April 1999 - Zero burning policy
- 10 june 2002 - ASEAN signed agreement on Transboundary smoke haze
- 25 nov 2003 - Agreement entered into force

## Acid deposition: When acidic substances formed in atmosphere reaches the earth's surface. It effects aquatic ecosystems and water mainly.

## Types of acid deposition:
1. Wet deposition: Substances reaches earth's surface through rain(pH < 5.6) or fog.
2. Dry deposition: Substances reaches earth's surface directly without precipitation.
