# Lecture 15

## Atmospheric Ozone (O3): It mainly occurs in 2 forms.
1. Stratospheric ozone - good ozone (90%)
2. Tropospheric ozone - bad ozone (10%)

## Ozone is measured in Dobson Units (DU) with global avg of approx 300 DU.

## Ozone depletion: It is general decline in total amount of ozone in stratosphere.

## Ozone hole: It is a seasonal decrease in ozone over a polar region which reduces to 220 DU.

## Ozone depleting substances: Human emitting gases containing at least one chlorine and bromine atom like CFCs, Halons, Methyl bromide, Methyl chloroform, etc.

## Catalytic destruction: Chlorine and Bromine participates in reactions that destroy ozone.

## Why Ozone hole severe over antarctica?
1. Very low temperature
2. Isolating polar airs
3. Sunlight returns in spring

## Vienna Convention and Montreal Protocol (Control Ozone Depletion)
- Vienna convention - 1985
- Montreal protocol signed - 1987
- Montreal protocol entered into force - 1989

## Indoor air pollution: It occurs inside buildings or houses where ventilation is poor and the major pollutants include tobacco smoke, formaldehyde, radon-222 and particulate matter.
