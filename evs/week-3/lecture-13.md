## Smog: Mixture of air pollutants that can form fog and reduce visibility. (Smoke + Fog)

## Types of Smog:
1. Industrial smog: Burning fossil fuels containing SO2.
2. Photochemical smog: Sunlight reacts with NO2 and Volatile Organic compounds (VOCs). It contains Ground level ozone, PAN, NO2, etc.

## Flue gas desulfurization (FGD): It removes Sulfur Dioxide (SO2) from exhaust gases. 

### It has 2 types: Wet FGD (Efficient and High Cost) and Dry FGD (Less efficient and Low cost).

## Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR): It reduces NOx from diesel engines.
