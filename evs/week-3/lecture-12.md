## Dispersion of air pollutant depends on:
1. Meteorological conditions
2. Emission height
3. Geographical features
4. Source type

## Lapse rate: It describes how air temperature changes with altitude.

## Types of Lapse rate:
1. Adiabatic lapse rate: The rate at which temperature of an air particle changes vertically.
- DALR: approx 1C per 100m or 10C per 1km.
- MALR: approx 0.6C per 100m or 6C per 1km.
2. Environmental lapse rate (ELR): The rate at which the surrounding atmosphere's temperature changes with altitude. Approx 6.5C change per km.

## Atmospheric stability: The atmosphere responds when an air particle is displaced vertically.
- ELR < DALR -> Stable
- ELR = DALR -> Neutral
- ELR > DALR -> Unstable

## Types of Inversion:
1. Radiation inversion - Develops at night
2. Subsidence inversion - Develops when large air mass present
3. Frontal inversion - Occurs when cold air moves below the warm air

## Air Quality Index (AQI): It is unitless index of measuring daily air quality. It is calculated by taking the highest pollutant sub index.
