# Lecture 7

## The 2030 Agenda: UN adopted the 2030 agenda for sustainable development in september 2015 with 17 goals and 169 targets.

## Important 17 Sustainable Goals
- SDG 6: Clean water and sanitation
- SDG 7: Affordable and clean energy
- SDG 12: Responsible consumption and production
- SDG 13: Climate action
- SDG 14: Life below water
- SDG 15: Life on land
- SDG 16: Peace, justice and humanity
- SDG 17: Partnerships for Goals 

## 5P's of SD
- People
- Planet
- Prosperity
- Peace
- Partnership
