## Six transformations for acheiving SDGs
1. Education, gender and inequality
2. Health, well-being and demography
3. Energy decarbonization
4. Sustainable food, land, water and oceans
5. Sustainable cities and communities
6. Digital revolution for sustainable development

## SDG Indicators: UN general assembly adopted the Global SDG indicators framework in July 2017 comprimising 232 indicators.

## SDG index score: It provides an overall assessment of how countries are progressing towards the 17 SDGs.

- SDG indicators measure specific aspects of progress
- SDG index score combines information to give overall assessment

## International spillovers: Actions of one country creating benefits or costs for other country affecting its ability to acheive SDGs.

### Types of international spillovers:
1. Trade-related environmental and social spillovers
2. Cross-border air and water flows
3. Economic and financial spillovers
4. Peacekeeping and security spillovers 

## Sustainablity indices for companies: It provides the company's environmental and social responsibility, alongside relevant economic and management practices.
