# Lecture 10
## Urban Heat Island Effect: The effect on urban area which produces more temperature than its surrounding areas.

## Green Building: A building which is constructed or deconstructed to reduce environment impact and use resources efficiently throughout its life cycle.

## Six principles of green building:
1. Siting and structure design
2. Energy efficiency
3. Water efficiency
4. Materials efficiency
5. Indoor environmental quality
6. Waste and toxics reduction

## Green roof: Living plants and vegetation installed on building roof.
### Two types of Green roof:
1. Intensive green roof: Deeper soil with large plants
2. Extensive green roof: Shallower soil with small plants

## Green wall: Vegetaion grown on walls of the building.

## Offgasing: Slow release of chemicals into air from toxic building materials.
## Deconstruction: Systematically dismantling the building to enable reuse or recyling its components.
