# Lecture 9

## Urbanization: Cities growing due to an increasing percentage of population comes to live in urban areas.

## How does cities grow?
1. Natural increase - births > deaths
2. Migration
3. Reclassification - town -> city

## Urbanization trends: 1950 - 2/3 of population lived in rural areas whereas in 2018 55% of population lives in urban areas.

## Megacity: Urban area with more than 10 million (1 crore) inhabitants.

## Urban sprawl: Rapid expansion of geograpic area of cities and towns and creating greater dependence on private vehicles or cars.

### It can be reduced through Smart Growth and New Urbanism.

## Ecocity: A city designed to reduce environmental problems.
## Smart city: A city to improve quality of life using information and technology.

## Sustainable mobility: Organizing the movement of people and goods to reduce fuel consumption and emissions.
