## Planetary boundaries: A safe operating space for humanity which helps us to perform human activities with boundaries by without harming the planet.

## Three pillars of sustainability:
1. Environmental sustainability
2. Social sustainability
3. Economic sustainability

## Important dates in development of sustainability
- First earth day in 1970
- Brundtland report (Definition of SD) in 1987
- Rio earth summit in 1992
- Millennium Development Goals in 2000 - 8 MDGs
- Sustainable Development Goals in 2015 - 17 SDGs
