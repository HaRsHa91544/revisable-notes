# Lecture 17

## Main Reason of Climate Change: Release of human made greenhouse gases like CO2, CH4, N20. The troposphere is warming while the upper atmosphere is cooling.

## The Keeling Curve: 
- It is a graph of atmospheric CO2 concentrations from 1958 named after scientist called Charles David Keeling. 
- It shows 2 patterns CO2 rises year by year and fluctates in a year due to plant growth, photosynthesis, etc. 
- The measurements are taken from Mauna Loa observatory and south pole. 
- Current 2025 CO2 reading is 425.73 ppm.

## Climate forcing:
### Incoming energy exceeds outgoing energy -> +ve energy -> Earth warms
### Outgoing energy exceeds incoming energy -> -ve energy -> Earth cools

## Aerosols: Tiny solid or liquid particles suspended in the atmosphere which can scatter or absorb sunlight and affect cloud formation. Sulfate aerosols(reflect sunlight), Carbon aerosols(absorb sunlight).

## Thermal inertia: Property of Oceans, absorb enormous heat and warm slowly.

## Climate lag: Continents warm relatively quickly than oceans which creates a climate lag.

## Global Warming Potential (GWP): Ratio between emitting a given mass of greenhouse gas with same mass of CO2 over a time period. 
- For 100 years, CO2 : 1, CH4 : 25-30, N20 : 298. 
- CO2 is still a major problem because human emit large quantities of it.

## Global Carbon Cycle:
### Fast domain: Carbon moves between atmosphere, oceans, vegetation over timescales ranging from decades to millennia.
### Slow domain: Carbon stored in rocks and sediments and they emit through volcanos, erosion, etc over 10000 years.

## Carbon source: A process which adds CO2 to the atmosphere. Ex: Burning fossil fuels.
## Carbon sink: A system that absorbs carbon from atmosphere. Ex: Forests and Oceans.
