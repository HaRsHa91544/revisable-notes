## How to reduce CO2 entering into the atmosphere?

## 1. Cleaner Energy: Improve energy efficiency, increase renewable energy use and use CCUS.

## Carbon, Capture, Utilization and Storage (CCUS)
1. Capture the CO2 from industries
2. Compress and transport them to suitable destination
3. Utilize or store it.

## Methods of capturing CO2:
1. Pre-combustion capture: CO2 is seperated before the fuel is burned.
2. Oxy-fuel combustion: Fuel is burned with pure oxygen which seperates CO2 and H20 and CO2 can be captured easily.
3. Post-combustion capture: CO2 is seperated after the combustion from exhaust gases. This process is useful and easily implementable.

## How CO2 is seperated from gas?
1. Absorption: CO2 dissolves into a liquid solvent often an amine.
2. Adsorption: CO2 accumulates on a surface.

## What happens after capture?
1. Pipelines are used to transport for large quantities.
2. Trucks and rails are used for some short distances.
3. Ships are used moving over large distances.
- And the captured CO2 is used in fertilizers, chemical production, food and beverages.

## Geological storage of CO2: The CO2 is stored in rocks which has required suitable conditions like:
1. Porosity: Enough space to hold CO2.
2. Permeability: Connected which allow CO2 to flow.
3. Cap rock: Preventing CO2 from escaping upward.

## Supercritical CO2: CO2 becomes supercritical when it is above 31.1C and 72.9atm. It has both properties of liquid and gas.

## Geoengineering: Large scale intervention on earth's natural systems to counteract climate change.
### It can be done in 2 ways:
1. Solar radiation management: Reflecting the small portion of sunlight back into space to reduce warming effect.
2. Carbon management: Remove CO2 from atmosphere or reduce its concentration.
