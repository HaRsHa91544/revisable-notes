# Lecture 16

## Weather: Short-term atmospheric conditions over hours or days.
## Climate: Long-term atmospheric conditions over 30 years minimum.

## The Greenhouse Effect: Sun radiation enters into earth and the dangerous UV rays are protected by OZONE and maintains some warmness on earth. The greenhouse gases are effecting the Ozone layer.

## Major greenhouse gases are: CO2, CH4, N2O, CFCs and water vapour. CFCs are artificial not natural.

## Climate changes types:
1. Natural change: Changes in solar activity leading to variations in Earth's orbit.
2. Artificial change: Burning coal, deforestation can leads to climatical changes.
### Climate action is recognized in SDG 13.

## Albedo: The fraction of incoming solar radiation reflected by surface or object. High Albedo: Snow and Ice reflect sunlight, Low Albedo: Dark surfaces absorb more sunlight.

## Evidences of Climate change: Rising in global temperature, Melting ice in Arctic and Antarctica, Changes in oceans and ecosystems.

## How do scientists study about past climates?
### Ice cores contain trapped air bubbles that preserve information about past atmospheres like greenhouse gases concentration.
