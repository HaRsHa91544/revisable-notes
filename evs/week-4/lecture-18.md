# Lecture 18

## Climate cascading: Changes in one system trigger effects in other connected systems.

## Polar Regions Melting: When Ice melts due to high albedo the water in ocean is darker absorbs more solar energy which causes more ice to melt.

## Permafrost: It is ground that remains frozen for long periods which contains stored carbon. When it melts decomposition releases CO2 and CH4 which causes additional warming. It effects mountain slopes and floods and also can spread surrounding lowlands.

## Global sea-level rise: The time-avg height of sea surface excluding short term changes.
### Two reasons for Sea level rise:
1. Thermal expansion: Seawater expands as it warms.
2. Land-ice loss

## Ocean acidification: Oceans absorb some of CO2 released by humans it helps in limiting the atmospheric CO2 but the ocean water becomes acidic due to the formation of Carbonic Acid. And the pH of ocean water decreases and may not be less than 7.
