# Lecture 20

## Adaption to climate change: Adjust to actual or expected climate change.
## Mitigation to climate change: Reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

## Three components of Adaption:
1. Reduce vulnerability - decrease how badly ecosystem effects
2. Enhance adaptive capacity - improve ability to prepare
3. Strengthen resilience

## Nature can help us adapt
1. Mangroves
2. Wetlands and floodplains
3. Forests and watershed

## International climate agreements
- IPCC (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change) established in 1988 -> provides scientific assessment of climate change
- UNFCC (United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change) in 1992
- Kyoto protocol in 1997 which sets emission reduction targets
- Paris agreement in 2015 which brings all countries

## Carbon pricing: It assigns cost to greenhouse gas emissions for companies to reduce them.
## Carbon credit: It allows companies to bring the remaining limit of carbon emissions from other companies.

## Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC)
1. It researches on causes of climate change and future projections. - Working Group 1 
2. It tells impacts, adaption, etc. - Working Group 2
3. It also tells about mitigation. - Working Group 3
