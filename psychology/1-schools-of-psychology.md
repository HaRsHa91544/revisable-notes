# Schools of Psychology (8 Marks Answer)

## Introduction

Psychology developed through different schools of thought. Each school attempted to explain human behavior and mental processes from a different perspective. The major schools of psychology are Structuralism, Functionalism, Behaviorism, Gestalt Psychology, Psychoanalysis, and Humanistic Psychology. 

---

## 1. Structuralism

Structuralism was the first scientific school of psychology. It focused on analyzing conscious experience by breaking it into its basic elements such as sensations, feelings, and perceptions.

**Key Idea:** Understanding the structure of the mind through its individual components. 

---

## 2. Functionalism

Functionalism focused on the purpose and functions of behavior and mental processes. It studied how thoughts and behaviors help individuals adapt to their environment.

**Key Idea:** Understanding the function and usefulness of behavior. 

---

## 3. Behaviorism

Behaviorism emphasized the study of observable behavior rather than internal mental processes. It explained behavior through learning, rewards, punishments, and environmental influences.

**Key Idea:** Observable behavior and learning. 

---

## 4. Gestalt Psychology

Gestalt psychologists argued that behavior and perception should be understood as a whole rather than as separate parts. They believed that the whole is greater than the sum of its parts.

**Key Idea:** Understanding the whole experience rather than isolated elements. 

---

## 5. Psychoanalysis

Developed by Sigmund Freud, Psychoanalysis emphasized the role of unconscious processes and childhood experiences in shaping human behavior.

**Key Idea:** Unconscious mind and childhood experiences influence behavior. 

---

## 6. Humanistic Psychology

Humanistic Psychology focused on personal growth, self-development, and achieving one's full potential. It emphasized positive aspects of human nature.

**Key Idea:** Personal growth and self-actualization. 

---

## Conclusion

The various schools of psychology contributed significantly to understanding human behavior. While Structuralism studied the structure of the mind, Functionalism examined its purpose, Behaviorism focused on observable actions, Gestalt Psychology emphasized the whole experience, Psychoanalysis explored unconscious influences, and Humanistic Psychology promoted personal growth and self-actualization. Together, these schools laid the foundation for modern psychology.
